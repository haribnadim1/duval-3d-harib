How a Duval smile is designed

Step 1 of 4

Photos and a conversation

It starts with photos and scans of your smile, and a conversation about what you'd like to change.

Step 2 of 4

Designed on screen

Your new smile is designed digitally around your face: the midline, the curve of your lips, the proportions of every tooth.

Step 3 of 4

Previewed before it's made

You see the design and adjust it until it feels like you. Nothing is made until you approve it.

Step 4 of 4

Then it becomes real

Only then is your smile crafted and fitted, with care and follow-up afterwards.

Questions we're often asked

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp

What is digital smile design?

A way of planning your new smile on screen using photos of your face and teeth, so you can see how it should look before treatment starts.

Can I see my new smile before treatment?

Yes, that's the point. You review and adjust the design with your dentist, and treatment only starts once you're happy with it.

How long do veneers last?

With good care, veneers can last many years. Your dentist will explain what to expect in your case.

I live outside the UAE. Can I get an opinion before I fly?

Yes. Send photos of your smile on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what we'd suggest and how many visits you might need.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with several insurers, including Daman and Thiqa. Cosmetic treatments like veneers usually aren't covered, but general dentistry may be.
